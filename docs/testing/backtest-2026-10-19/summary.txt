Asumsi dan batasan:
- USDT = USD; fixed conversion at BI USD midpoint 2026-10-19 (18142.26 + 17961.74)/2; IDR FX fluctuations excluded
- Spot fees maker/taker 0.1%; market/stop slippage 2 bps; no tax or withdrawal fees
- Current public exchange rules, not historical snapshots; no parameter optimization on test period
- EMA 1h signals with 1m execution; grid/DCA/MM 1m; cold-start indicator warmup within tested year
- Default risk: 1% per trade, daily kill 5%, peak drawdown kill 20%, close on kill; no automatic reset
- Grid fixed initial-open +/-15%, 10 levels, 10 USDT/level, stop 3% below lower boundary; does not restart after stop
- OHLC limit fills omit queue position, liquidity and latency; MM/grid results are approximate and potentially optimistic
- Final equity includes open inventory marked at final close; closing fees on remaining inventory not deducted
- Fees metric includes closed trades only; entry fees on open inventory affect equity but may not appear in metric
- Five coins selected from current top ten, not a historical-universe study; selection/survivorship bias applies
